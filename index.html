Our Story

A lifelong home in the heart of Kadapa

Aarti Home is a nonprofit organization caring for girls of all ages — from newborn babies to young women — in a spacious, eco-friendly campus on the outskirts of Kadapa. Thousands of girls have passed through the portals of Aarti Home since its inception in 1992. Our girls are cared for by dedicated house mothers in nurturing family units where they find safety and comfort in a community of sisterhood.

Our Impact

Over the years, our girls have blossomed into confident, responsible young women with their hearts in the right place. Aarti Home is a lifelong refuge, where they receive ongoing support and return to celebrate milestones. Currently, 132 girls reside in Aarti Home.

Enabling meaningful journeys

From our home to the world

Sivajyothi’s journey of crafting dreams into reality

Sivajyothi came to Aarti Home at the age of 6, where she found a nurturing environment to grow and learn. Through education and vocational training at Aarti Community College, she gained the skills and confidence to pursue her dreams. Today she is a Commis II chef at a leading hotel in Bengaluru, turning her hard work into a remarkable career.

Soundarya’s journey: rising through learning and resilience

When Soundarya lost both her parents at the age of 8, she found a new home at Aarti Home, where learning and guidance became her anchor. Starting with just Grade 2 in Telugu, she quickly mastered English and excelled in mathematics. Determined to become a software engineer, she prepared for competitive exams and earned a place at Freshworks.

Chitti’s story: from timid beginnings to a bright tomorrow

When Chitti arrived at Aarti Home at just 5 years old, she was a quiet, anxious child grieving the loss of her father. Surrounded by care and encouragement, she gradually found her confidence and love for learning. Today she is a joyful Grade 5 student at Aarti School, excelling academically while exploring dance and painting.

A longstanding partnership

Providing Possibilities Foundation (PPF)

The relationship between the Providing Possibilities Foundation (PPF) and Aarti for Girls began in 1992 with the founding of Aarti Home by Sandhya Puchalapalli. In the early years, PPF founder Roopa Reddy worked alongside Sandhya, helping with fundraising and building a network of supporters who believed in Aarti’s mission. After PPF was established as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, it became an important channel for donor support.

As Aarti expanded to include Aarti Village, Aarti School, and other initiatives, PPF remained a committed partner. Roopa continues to support the mission as a member of the Aarti for Girls board, while the PPF community remains actively engaged in advancing opportunities for girls and women. Today, PPF funds 20% of the food costs for all children across Aarti’s programs, helping ensure that every child receives nutritious meals in a safe and nurturing environment.

For more than two decades, this partnership has reflected a shared commitment to creating brighter futures through education, wellbeing, and empowerment.
